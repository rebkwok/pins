Handling orders

New orders

Every new order is emailed to the addresses in the shop settings. The email lists each item and who sends it: dispatchers are only emailed about the products set to email them, so pass the rest on (the email says when there's anything to pass on). Items of products without a dispatcher are sent by the shop team.

To keep track:

  • Orders: filter by status, "Has items to ship or collect", dispatcher or product, and download them to Excel.
  • Dispatcher activity: who has items to send, oldest first. Items waiting more than {{ overdue_days }} days are highlighted.

Items that don't need sending (e.g. raffle tickets) are shown as "No shipping needed". An order with nothing to send is completed as soon as it's paid.

Sending and collecting items

Dispatchers mark their items as sent on their Items to send page. You can also mark any order's items: on the order's page, tick the items, then "Mark selected items as shipped" (or collected, for collection orders), and confirm.

The customer is emailed each time, with the items just sent and any still to come, so mark items as they go, not before.

Collection orders are by prior arrangement: the customer should have contacted PINS before ordering, and their emails ask them to contact PINS to arrange when and where to collect.

Items that won't be sent

If an item can't or won't be sent (e.g. it's out of stock, or the customer no longer wants it), tick it on the order's page, then "Mark selected items as won't be sent". The customer isn't emailed: contact them, and refund the item if necessary (see Refunds).

Undoing a mistake

If an item was marked shipped (or collected) or won't be sent by mistake, press "Undo" by its status on the order's page: it's ready to send again. The customer isn't emailed, so if they were told it had been sent, you may want to contact them. Items can't be changed once the order is completed, refunded or cancelled.

Completing an order

Once all its items have been sent (or won't be), "Mark as completed" on the order's page. The customer is emailed that the order is complete. Orders aren't completed automatically.

Refunds

  • A whole order: "Refund" on the order's page. The payment is refunded in full, the customer is emailed, and any items not yet sent can be marked won't be sent at the same time.
  • Part of an order (e.g. one item): ask whoever looks after PINS's Stripe account to refund that amount in the Stripe dashboard (Payments, then the payment, then Refund). It's recorded on the order, and the customer is emailed, automatically.

Refunds take 5–10 working days to reach the customer. Refunds (and cancelling) don't change stock: if items are going back on sale, update their stock in Shop stock.

Disputes

A dispute (or chargeback) is when a customer asks their bank to reverse a payment, e.g. saying the order never arrived, or they didn't make it. The bank takes the money back from PINS's Stripe account straight away, and Stripe charges a dispute fee.

When it happens:

  • The shop is emailed "payment disputed", with the reason, the amount, and the date to respond by; the same is noted on the order.
  • Whoever looks after PINS's Stripe account responds in the Stripe dashboard (Payments, then Disputes) before that date, with evidence: e.g. proof of postage or delivery, emails with the customer, the order's details. Or they can accept the dispute, if the customer is right.
  • Don't refund a disputed payment: the money has already been taken back.
  • It's often worth contacting the customer: a misunderstanding can be sorted out, and the customer can withdraw the dispute.

When the bank decides (which can take weeks), the shop is emailed "dispute closed", saying whether it was won (the money comes back) or lost, and it's noted on the order.

Cancelling an unpaid order

Only orders that haven't been paid can be cancelled ("Cancel order" on the order's page); the customer isn't emailed. To cancel a paid order, refund it.

Contacting customers

The customer's email address and phone number are on the order's page. "Customer's order page" shows the order as the customer sees it (the page linked from their emails), e.g. to send them the link again. Replies to the shop's emails go to the reply-to address in the shop settings.

Products and stock

Products and variants

A product (e.g. a T-shirt) has one or more variants: what customers actually choose and buy (e.g. Black, M). Variants can differ by colour, size or name, and can have their own price (leave it blank for the product's price) and image. Products with one variant don't show a choice.

Untick "Live" to hide a product, or a variant, from the shop. Variants that have been ordered can't be deleted (so past orders keep them): untick Live instead.

Stock

  • The stock shown is what's still available: items in customers' baskets have already been taken off it (they go back if the basket expires or the item is removed). Because baskets change it while you're editing, your change is added to the current stock when you save (e.g. changing 10 to 12 adds 2), so nothing customers did meanwhile is lost.
  • Shared stock: for variants sold from the same pile, e.g. single cards and packs of 5. Tick "Variants share stock", set the product's stock (in single items), and each variant's pack size: a pack of 5 uses 5.
  • No stock limit: for things that can't run out, e.g. competition entries or items made to order. Stock isn't counted.
  • Refunds and cancelled orders don't put items back in stock: update it by hand if they're going back on sale.

Shipping and dispatchers

  • Each product has a shipping rate (Shop admin > Shipping rates). A basket is charged the highest of its products' rates, once. New products start with the default rate. Collection is free.
  • Untick "Needs shipping" for products that aren't posted or collected (e.g. competition entries): they have no shipping cost, and an order of only these is completed once paid.
  • A product's dispatcher sends it (or holds it, for collection); tick "Email the dispatcher about new orders" to email them about each order. Changing the dispatcher moves the product's unsent items in existing orders to the new one. Products without a dispatcher are sent by the shop team.

Images and categories

Add images on the product's page: the first is shown on the shop page, and all of them (with the variants' own images) on the product's page.

Categories are the buttons on the shop page. Drag them into order in Shop stock > Categories, and drag a category's products into order on the category's page.

Sales

A sale takes a percentage off chosen products, or whole categories, between its start and end dates, with an optional banner on every shop page. Only one sale can run at a time, and it's only shown if it has products or categories.

Discount codes

  • A code takes a fixed amount off the whole basket, including shipping, and works with sale prices.
  • Each code can be used once. Leave the code blank for a random one; untick "Active" to stop it being used.
  • If it's more than the basket's total, the order is free, and what's left on the code is lost. It can't leave less than £0.30 to pay (Stripe's minimum), so a basket like that can't use it.

FAQs, settings and activity

  • Shop FAQs (e.g. delivery and returns) are on the FAQs page, linked from the shop, products, basket and checkout. Drag them into order.
  • Shop settings: who's emailed about new orders, the reply-to address for the shop's emails, and the footer on emails to customers (with the charity number).
  • Shop activity shows who changed what in the shop, and when.

For dispatchers

{% endif %}

Your items to send

Dispatchers send (or hold, for collection) some of the shop's products. Items to send lists your items in paid orders, oldest orders first, with the customer's name and address (or, for collection, their phone number and email). If a product is set to email you, you're emailed about each order with it too. "Download (Excel)" gives you the list as a spreadsheet.

Marking items sent

When you've posted an order's items, press "Mark as shipped" on the order, and confirm. The customer is emailed, so do it once they've gone, not before. It marks only your items in the order: other dispatchers mark theirs.

For a collection order, the customer arranges with PINS when and where to collect. Hand the items over when they do, then press "Mark as collected".

An order marked "Outside the UK" needs shipping arranged with the shop first: check with them before sending it.

The Sent tab shows what you've sent (the latest 100 items).

If something goes wrong

If you can't send something (e.g. it's out of stock or damaged), or you marked an order by mistake, tell the shop team (reply to the order email): they can mark items as won't be sent, or undo marking them sent.
